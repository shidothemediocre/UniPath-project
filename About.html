About UniPath

Helping students discover the perfect university and major with confidence.

Mission & Vision

At UniPath, our mission is to help students easily discover the right university and major for their future.

We envision a world where higher education choices are simple, accessible, and empowering for every student.

Key Features

  • Search universities instantly
  • Compare universities side-by-side
  • Explore majors and degree programs
  • View university locations
  • Personalized recommendations
  • Responsive design for all devices

Data & Transparency

At UniPath, we provide trusted university information from official and reliable sources, helping students make clear and confident decisions about their future.

We continuously update our data and welcome feedback to keep our platform accurate and transparent.

Who We Serve

  • High School Graduates
  • Transfer Students
  • Students Exploring Majors
  • Parents & Guardians

Developer

UniPath is developed by a group of students from the University of Information Technology (UIT) as part of their Web Technology subject group project.

The project combines creativity, teamwork, and web development skills to create a useful platform that helps students explore universities and make informed decisions about their educational future.
