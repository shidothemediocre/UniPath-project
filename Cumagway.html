About the University

The University of Computer Studies (Magway) was located in Ayemyathaya Village, and was moved to a new campus near Magyikan Village on the Magway-Taungdwingyi Road in the 2012–2013 academic year. The university, administered by the Ministry of Education, offers undergraduate and master degree programs in computer science and technology.
