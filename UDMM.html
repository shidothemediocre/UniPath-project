About the University

"University of Dental Medicine, Mandalay 🦷 is one of Myanmar's premier higher education institutions dedicated to dental medicine and oral healthcare. Established in 1998, it plays a vital role alongside its Yangon counterpart in producing skilled dental practitioners, surgeons, and specialists through comprehensive undergraduate and postgraduate programs, clinical training, scientific research, and community health services."
