About the University

"University of Dental Medicine, Yangon 🦷 is Myanmar's premier institution for higher dental education and oral healthcare. Established in 1964, it is dedicated to producing skilled dental surgeons and specialists through comprehensive academic programs, advanced clinical training, innovative research, and vital public oral health services."
