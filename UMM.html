About the University

**University of Medicine, Mandalay 🏥** is one of Myanmar's premier and oldest medical universities. Located in the cultural heart of Upper Myanmar, it is dedicated to cultivating exceptional doctors and healthcare specialists through high-standard medical education, extensive clinical training at major regional hospitals, and a steadfast commitment to research and public health service.
