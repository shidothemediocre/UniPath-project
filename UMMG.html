About the University

University of Medicine, Magway 🏥** is one of Myanmar's premier public medical universities. Located in central Myanmar, it is dedicated to educating competent medical doctors and specialists through comprehensive medical education, practical clinical training at regional teaching hospitals, and an active commitment to community healthcare and scientific research.
