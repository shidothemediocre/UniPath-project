About the University

**University of Medicine, Taunggyi 🏥** is one of Myanmar's key regional medical universities. Situated in the scenic Shan State, it is dedicated to nurturing skilled doctors and healthcare professionals through rigorous medical education, hands-on clinical training at major teaching hospitals, and a strong commitment to community healthcare services.
