About the University

University of Pharmacy, Mandalay 💊 is Upper Myanmar's premier institution for pharmaceutical education and research. Located in Amarapura Township, Mandalay, it is dedicated to educating proficient pharmacists and pharmaceutical specialists through comprehensive academic training, practical laboratory research, and a strong commitment to quality medication management and public healthcare services.
