About the University

University of Pharmacy, Yangon 💊 is Myanmar's premier institution for pharmaceutical education and research. Situated in Yangon, it is dedicated to cultivating skilled pharmacists and pharmaceutical scientists through specialized academic programs, hands-on laboratory training, and a strong commitment to quality drug management, scientific research, and national healthcare services.
