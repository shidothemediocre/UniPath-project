About the University

Yangon University of Economics (YUEco) is a state university under the Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education, Myanmar. The university specializes in economics, statistics, commerce, management studies, and applied economics. It provides undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral programmes and aims to produce qualified graduates who can contribute to business, government, and society.
