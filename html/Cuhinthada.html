About the University

Government Computer College (GCC) was founded in September 2001. In 2007, it was reformed as the University of Computer Studies, Hinthada (UCSH). Located in Hinthada Township, Ayeyarwady Region, Myanmar, UCSH is one of the higher educational institutions under the Ministry of Science and Technology, Myanmar. The university provides degrees in computer science and computer technology to satisfy human resource demands both at home and abroad.
