About the University

UCS-Mtla has been producing graduates in computer sciences and technology majors called CS and CT. It is a place with no-limits for opportunities regarding information technological knowledge and professions for students, as well as a place for teaching, research, service, and innovation with the latest technology and IT-based applications to improve the lives of people in Meiktila, Myanmar.
