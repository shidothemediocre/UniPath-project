About the University

University of Computer Studies (Pakokku) is a public undergraduate university. Students study various computer disciplines, including hardware, networking, programming, imaging, and artificial intelligence. The university's main three-story building is situated on the north side of the Pakokku-Pauk Road, 6 miles and 6 furlongs from Pakokku Town near Tingat Village, spanning across 62.5 acres.
