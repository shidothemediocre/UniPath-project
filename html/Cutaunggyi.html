About the University

The University of Computer Studies (Taunggyi) is situated in Southern Shan State, Myanmar. Established on August 15, 2000, as Government Computer College (Taunggyi), it was upgraded to university status on January 20, 2007. Located west of the Union Road connecting Taunggyi and Hopone, it is 4 miles from the capital city of Taunggyi and spans across 215 acres.
