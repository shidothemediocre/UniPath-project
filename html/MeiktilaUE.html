About the University

Meiktila University of Economics is one of Myanmar's premier specialized institutions dedicated to higher education in economics, business administration, and commerce. Established to support regional development and advance academic standards in Upper Myanmar, the university offers diverse undergraduate and postgraduate programs focused on cultivating skills in management, accounting, statistics, and applied economics. Through its commitment to academic quality, research, and practical learning, it plays a pivotal role in nurturing ethical professionals, innovative entrepreneurs, and strategic leaders capable of driving sustainable economic growth.
