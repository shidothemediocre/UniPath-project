About the University

Monywa University of Economics is a prominent public higher education institution in Upper Myanmar specializing in economics, business administration, and commerce. Committed to academic excellence and regional development, the university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs designed to equip students with practical knowledge in management, statistics, and applied economics. Through its focus on quality education, research, and leadership development, plays a vital role in producing skilled professionals and entrepreneurs to support Myanmar's economic growth.
