About the University

The National University of Arts and Culture, Mandalay (NUAC Mandalay) is a prominent public higher education institution dedicated to preserving and promoting Myanmar's rich cultural heritage. Operating under the Ministry of Religious Affairs and Culture, the university offers specialized undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs in five core artistic disciplines: Music, Dramatic Arts & Dance, Painting, Sculpture, and Cinema & Theatre. Unlike conventional academic universities that rely strictly on high school matriculation scores, NUAC Mandalay evaluates candidates through practical auditions and talent evaluations to nurture specialized, high-caliber artists and cultural preservationists for the nation.
