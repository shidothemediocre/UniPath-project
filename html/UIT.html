About the University

The University of Information Technology (UIT), Yangon, is one of Myanmar's leading universities specializing in computer science, information technology, and digital innovation. The university is dedicated to producing highly skilled software engineers, network engineers, cybersecurity specialists, AI researchers, and IT professionals through quality education, research, and industry collaboration.
